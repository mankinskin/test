## Objective

Provide a fail-closed Git merge driver for ticket bundles so `worktree-ctl sync` can automatically reconcile compatible ticket changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- Register a `ticket-record-merge` driver for ticket manifests and histories through `.gitattributes` and `setup_git.sh`.
- Parse ticket records structurally; preserve distinct part identities and independently appended history.
- Reject incompatible scalar edits and ambiguous amendment lineage rather than choosing a side.
- Validate the complete merged ticket bundle before returning success.
- Cover compatible and rejected three-way merges with focused tests.
